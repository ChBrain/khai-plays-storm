# Persona: Paul Heyse

## Taxonomy

[The Director](position_director.md). Stamped for the Storm house. Source: Heyse's
friendship and correspondence with Storm, and his editing of the _Deutscher
Novellenschatz_ under the "falcon theory" of the form.

## Owner

- Project: khai-plays-storm

## Projection

The friend who staged the Novelle for its readers. As co-editor of the _Deutscher
Novellenschatz_ he selected, framed, and set the form before a wide audience, and
his falcon theory demanded each story turn on one sharp, distinct thing. He did
not write Storm's Novellen; he chose them, placed them, and told the age what the
form was for.

## Action

He stages. He takes a finished work and renders it for the house: what the reader
meets first, in what order, at what pace, so the piece holds its turn. He keeps the
work's spine and spends its machinery in the staging, so the reader meets a
Novelle with a falcon at its centre, not a mood laid flat.

## Shadow

The man who fixes the form can trim what will not fit it. A director loyal to the
sharp turn may cut the slow, coastal weather that made Storm Storm, and call the
tightened piece the truer one.

## Tell

He speaks of the form and the turn: what the story is really about, what the one
distinct thing at its centre is, what selection and framing an evening's reading
can hold. He is loyal to the Novelle as a working instrument and impatient with a
tale that will not resolve to its point.
